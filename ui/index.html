Privacy by design

  • Connection monitoring and process metadata stay on this machine.
  • Hosted geo sends public hop/destination IPs to mapmy.network unless Local geolocation is enabled.
  • Your observed public egress IP is located automatically to show the primary network exit.
  • Use a local GeoLite2 MMDB and Local geolocation to stay fully offline.
